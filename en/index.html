How this result is calculated

  • We apply a single price gap to your total monthly budget, with no category-by-category breakdown.
  • Gap applied: the same lifestyle in Mexico costs about 33.4% less than in Quebec — for example $2,864 there for a $4,300 budget here.
  • Exchange rate used: CAD 1 = 13.29 MXN at the market rate, compared with 8.85 MXN at equal purchasing power.
  • Sources: World Bank purchasing power parities (2025) and the Bank of Canada's daily exchange rate.
  • Figures last updated: October 30, 2026.
  • What the estimate does not include: flights, travel insurance, temporary-stay costs and other expenses specific to your situation, unless stated otherwise.
  • The result varies by city and lifestyle in Mexico. It is an estimate for comparison, not a spending promise.
